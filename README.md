### Objective
The objective of this project is to model and simulate the propagation of heat in a metal plate over time.

Initialy, the metal plate will be at room temperature. One end wll be heated, and we will observe how the heat is distribuited throughout the plate.

This phisical phenomenon is known as thermal conduction.
